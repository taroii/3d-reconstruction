## Thresholds, fixed in advance

| Outcome | Condition |
|---|---|
| GO | `FP_model - FP_GT >= 0.05` (5 pp) **and** `FP_model >= 2.0x FP_GT`, 95% bootstrap CI on the paired difference excludes 0, for >=1 stream on Tier A, **and** the sign is stable across the full (eta, tau, beta) sweep |
| WEAK | CI excludes 0 but the effect is below the GO thresholds, or the sign flips anywhere in the sweep |
| NO-GO | CI includes 0, **or** `FP_GT >= 15%`, **or** the Sec. 4 gate fails on every dataset |

Centre of the sweep: eta=0.05, tau=2, beta=0.2.
Sweep: eta in [0.02, 0.05, 0.1], tau in [1, 2, 3], beta in [0.1, 0.2, 0.3].
Unit of analysis: the scene. Test: Wilcoxon signed-rank, paired bootstrap CI
(10000 resamples), Holm-corrected across streams.

A result landing near a boundary is WEAK. Thresholds are not to be adjusted.
